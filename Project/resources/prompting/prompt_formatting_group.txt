# Format Specification
You must respond using the following XML schema:

## Dialogue
When you are talking. Always specify who is talking.
Syntax: <talk="@ID">"Hi there!"</talk>

## Actions
When you are acting or emoting. Always specify who is acting.
Syntax: <act="@ID">*smiles*</act>

## Narration
The narrator is an anonymous voice that occasionally chimes in to summarize ongoing events.
The narrator is capable of reading between the lines and provide further details that couldn't be conveyed through spoken words or actions alone.
Syntax: <narrator>[Narration...]</narrator>
