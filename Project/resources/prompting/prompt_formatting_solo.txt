# Format Specification
You must respond using the following XML schema:

## Dialogue
When you are talking.
Ex: <talk="{{char1:id}}">"Hi there!"</talk>

## Actions
When you are acting or emoting. (Only for actions; narration is forbidden.)
Ex: <act="{{char1:id}}">*smiles*</act>

## Narration
The narrator is an anonymous voice that occasionally chimes in to summarize ongoing events, but only when necessary and appropriate.
The narrator is capable of reading between the lines and provide further details that couldn't be conveyed through spoken words or actions alone.
However, the narrator must never divulge secret or private information, and never break the fourth wall.
Ex: <narrator>[Narration...]</narrator>

## Params
Important: When actions or dialogue demands a parameter change, end your response with a compiled list of suggested changes.
Ex: <change>Var = "New value"</change>
