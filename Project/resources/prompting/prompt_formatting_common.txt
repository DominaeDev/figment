# Format Specification
You must respond using the following XML schema:

## Dialogue
When you are talking.
Syntax: <talk="@ID">"Hi there!"</talk>

## Actions
When you are acting or emoting.
Syntax: <act="@ID">*smiles*</act>

## Narration
The Narrator is an anonymous voice that will occasionally chime in to provide additional details and exposition about {{char}} that couldn't otherwise be conveyed through words and actions.
Syntax: <narrator>[Expository text here.]</narrator>

# Table of participants (ID, Name)
"@{{id_char1}}" = "{{char1}}"
"@{{id_char2}}" = "{{char2}}"
"@{{id_user}}" = "{{user}}"
